# Grilled Paneer with Garlic Yoghurt Sauce

![Grilled Paneer with Garlic Yoghurt Sauce](../images/grilled-paneer-with-garlic-yoghurt-sauce.png)

## Nutrition

- Calories: ~680 kcal
- Protein: ~37 g
- Carbohydrates: ~28 g
- Fat: ~45 g

## Ingredients

### For the grilled paneer
- 150–200 g paneer, cut into thin slices
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/4 tsp black pepper powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- 1–2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp oil

### For the garlic pepper sauce
- 1/2 tsp butter
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 3–4 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp black pepper powder
- 1/2 tsp mixed herbs
- 1 cup thick curd / greek yogurt 
- Fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 tsp honey
- Salt to taste

### To serve
- Salad of your choice (lettuce, cucumber, tomato, onion, etc.)

## Steps

1. Slice the paneer into thin pieces.
2. Mix turmeric, red chilli powder, coriander powder, black pepper, garam masala, salt, and lemon juice.
3. Rub this masala mixture well onto the paneer slices and let it sit for atleast 15 minutes.
4. Heat a pan with 1 tsp oil and cook the paneer on both sides until lightly crisp.
5. In another pan, add butter.
6. Add the chopped onion and crushed garlic, then sauté until light golden brown.
7. Add black pepper powder and mixed herbs. Let the mixture cool slightly.
8. Add the thick curd/yoghurt, fresh coriander, honey, and salt. Mix well to make the sauce.
9. Serve the grilled paneer hot with the garlic pepper sauce and some fresh salad on the side.

## Notes
- Use thick curd or Greek yogurt for a creamier sauce.
- Let the onion-garlic mixture cool a bit before adding curd so it does not split.
- You can add extra salad or 1 roti on the side to make it a fuller meal.
